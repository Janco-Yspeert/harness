# As-Built — Spike 014j

## Implemented shape

The final implementation at candidate commit `93ade31b1dcb6487798b27e812dc443fce30af43` adds a root-authorized prepared candidate-observation operation and separates provider launch selection from exact-model attestation in the generic governed-executor path.

`GovernedHost.prepareCandidateObservation` accepts only the workflow, active implementation candidate, evaluator revision and revision identity, private inventory identity, and procedure selector. The HTTP surface is under the existing authenticated human/root boundary and is not exposed through the worker action protocol. Before launch, the host checks the active committed implementation handoff and evaluation-readiness record, reads the committed coverage map by identity, resolves the configured evaluator-private workspace, validates the frozen inventory and selected procedure material, and reconstructs the exact committed candidate `evaluator-verify` methodology, skill, contract, contract-source identity, capabilities, workspaces, and host actions.

Preparation reuses the existing candidate-subject runner and `containedLaunch` boundary. Only the selected frozen procedure tests and support files are copied to a temporary read-only procedure root. The host also creates read-only topology and before-state inputs. The subject sees the reconstructed repository, disposable evaluation workspace, selected procedure material, and host inputs; its writable roots remain the workspaces permitted by the reconstructed contract plus scratch. The evidence root remains outside subject visibility and writability. Candidate results and tool activity are captured by the non-authoritative subject relay and do not become workflow authority.

The inner subject bundle records the exact candidate composition and runtime commit, evaluator revision/inventory/procedure/material identities, topology, before/after observations, process outcome, stdout/stderr, ordered worker-tool exchanges, reconstruction facts, and containment facts. Validation rejects incomplete or truncated streams, missing, changed, duplicate or unbound artifacts, symlinks, and missing or malformed frozen-procedure and host-input bindings. A valid bundle is made read-only, copied beneath `.eval/prepared-observations/<observation>/bundle/` in the evaluator-private workspace, and independently revalidated.

`kernel.prepared-observation` is the durable public-safe lifecycle record. Its deterministic observation identity covers the record facts other than the identity field itself. A sealed record binds the workflow, non-authoritative human/root purpose, candidate composition, runtime, evaluator revision/inventory/procedure, and private bundle-manifest identity while exposing only a fixed relative location template. A failed subject run produces an explicit failed record without a resolvable bundle. Record insertion is transaction-serialized and idempotent for an identical identity; conflicting content is rejected. Trusted consumers resolve a sealed record inside the configured private workspace, recompute the outer and inner identities, and reject traversal, substitution, tampering, lifecycle mismatch, or record/bundle binding mismatch.

The executor model now treats profile `model` and workflow-grant `model` as provider launch selectors, with the grant selector retaining precedence. A separate optional `exactModel` flows from workflow authority into the Role Grant and execution provenance. Execution provenance records the selected profile, requested launch values, required exact model, enforcement flags, provider-confirmed values, and attestation availability separately. Without `exactModel`, a provider-reported concrete model is recorded without comparison to the selector. With `exactModel`, the exact value replaces the selector for launch, adapters without model attestation are refused before launch, provider mismatch fails the execution, and result submission remains blocked until matching provider confirmation is observed. The same launch-planning rules apply through the registered Claude and Codex adapter abstraction; no model-family alias table or Claude-only equivalence was added.

Visible coverage exercises the root-only closed request boundary, active handoff and readiness bindings, material and input identity failures, private-data non-disclosure, read-only host inputs, topology distinctions, tamper detection, non-authoritative lifecycle behavior, trusted resolution, selector precedence, alias-safe attestation, exact-model mismatch, unavailable attestation, and both registered adapter shapes. The final accepted evaluator run recorded all thirteen criteria as satisfied. Its clean candidate checks passed typecheck, lint, formatting, the mandatory executable cases, and 244 of 245 repository tests; the remaining 014e containment failure reproduced at the pre-implementation baseline in that evaluation environment.
