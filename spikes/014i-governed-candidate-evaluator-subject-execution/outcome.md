## What Was Established

Harness can reconstruct a candidate evaluator from exact committed bytes,
execute it as an observational subject inside host-owned disposable
containment, terminate its worker-tool calls locally, and retain raw evidence
without granting the subject authority over verification, promotion, trust, or
workflow advancement. Sealed evidence can then be handed read-only to the
trusted evaluator.

## Implementation Summary

The implementation reuses the committed-revision methodology builder and the
014h containment primitive. It creates disjoint repository, evaluation,
scratch, and forbidden roots; enforces reconstructed workspace modes; captures
stdout, stderr, worker-tool exchanges, process data, and host-side
before/after observations; and fail-closes sealing on missing, altered,
truncated, substituted, or unsafe evidence. Candidate host actions are
confined to disposable destinations, while human requests are unavailable and
authoritative effects are denied.

## Evaluation Evidence

Independent verification of the exact candidate passed all 10 acceptance
criteria, both executable cases, all four manual reviews, and the clean
candidate regression (`234/234`). As-Built found no Missing, Contradictory, or
Extra discrepancies. The accepted evidence is the promoted, identity-bound
record above; it includes the complete attempt history and both evaluator
revisions.

The ordinary promotion plan was `INELIGIBLE` because evaluator v14 treated
frozen grader material as requiring a separate disclosure decision. Complete
evidence recovery was performed under the recorded `PROMOTION_POLICY_DEFECT`
path without reconstructing or omitting evidence. This is preserved as a
limitation and does not change the verification PASS.

## Material History

The first evaluation revision was blocked by an evaluator defect: its bundle
oracle over-constrained representations left open by the frozen authority.
After an integrity-checked evaluator repair, attempts 002 and 003 found
implementation failures in contract-file identity binding and duplicated
methodology reconstruction. The implementation corrected both issues. A
separate protected-Claude runtime repair was required to make the evaluator
execution reliable; it did not change the candidate or frozen evaluation
authority. Attempt 004 then passed without evaluator drift or post-implementation
evaluator correction.

## Decisions

The candidate-subject boundary is evidence-only: a candidate evaluator cannot
evaluate itself or advance the authoritative workflow. The accepted mechanism
is authorized for successor work, beginning with the already-frozen 014g
evaluation. The promotion eligibility defect is not endorsed as the desired
architecture.

## Discoveries

Exact committed methodology reconstruction and host-owned raw capture are
necessary to make candidate-composition evaluation attributable. Summary-only
subject output is insufficient. The evaluator should own evaluation facts,
while ordinary post-PASS archival policy belongs to Harness; otherwise a valid
PASS can require bespoke recovery merely to preserve its evidence.

## Deferred Concerns

014i does not establish the 014g transition, 014f retirement, the 014e live
canary, or later methodology changes. Successor work should simplify the
promotion/recovery boundary, retain hidden evaluator history without conflating
it with maintained project regressions, and tighten the trusted-N to candidate
handoff.
