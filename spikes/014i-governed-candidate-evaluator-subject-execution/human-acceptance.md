### Third: Spike 014j — N → N+1 authority and evaluator tightening

014j should deliberately address the authority defects exposed across 014d–014i rather than adding another local recovery.

Its intended direction is:

1. **Trusted N remains the sole authority evaluating candidate N+1.** Candidate N+1 may be executed only as a bounded non-authoritative subject where its proposed runtime composition itself must be observed.
2. **Candidate methodology cannot approve, promote or otherwise establish trust in itself.** Final methodology adoption remains a separate, explicit human trust decision bound to the exact N-authored PASS and exact candidate methodology identity.
3. **The evaluator owns evaluation facts, not archival policy.** It should produce immutable canonical evidence and an exact identity-bound archive inventory/plan, not decide whether ordinary evaluator evidence is `ELIGIBLE` or `INELIGIBLE` for archival.
4. **Harness owns ordinary post-PASS archival deterministically.** Trusted methodology/policy declares the trigger, source and destination; the host validates the exact evidence bytes and performs the archive without requiring a second model decision or routine human disclosure approval.
5. **Hidden evaluator material remains hidden from evaluated roles during the active evaluation cycle, but hidden status alone is not a permanent nondisclosure classification.** Canonical evaluator evidence may be archived under `evaluation/` after PASS without being transformed into ordinary public project tests.
6. **Regression curation remains separate from evaluator archival.** Publishing/adapting an evaluator discovery as a maintained project regression is a distinct operation and must not be confused with historical evidence archival.
7. **Tighten the N → N+1 handoff against the one-generation lag exposed by 014d.** Exact candidate composition, trusted predecessor, independent PASS, archival state and final human trust authority should form one explicit forward-only chain with no second centre of authority.
8. **Retain compatibility only where needed to close existing v14 workflows.** The v2 `ELIGIBLE/INELIGIBLE` promotion-plan model and complete-archive recovery should not silently become permanent architecture merely because they exist.
9. **Do not broaden 014j into provider SDK, quota, caching or session-resumption work.** Those remain separate concerns.

014j should be independently evaluated under the methodology trusted at its start and should leave a cleaner authority model, not another bootstrap-only route.

Preserve the valuable permanent primitives: host-owned containment, exact trusted methodology reconstruction, host-mediated evidence mutation, bounded candidate-subject execution, explicit workflow authority and accepted grant-retirement behavior.

### Finally: complete the external-project live canary under current authority

Prefer a fresh current-authority workflow/cycle rather than pretending an old pinned grant proves the cleaned architecture. The final question should be operationally simple:

> Can current Harness govern a real external project end to end without bespoke recovery work or manual reconstruction of its authority transitions?
